About Me

I'm Sushil Kumar, a Contact Center Engineer with 5+ years of experience at Cognizant, where I work as a Senior Associate designing and delivering enterprise-grade IVR systems for large-scale banking and financial services.

I specialise in Amazon Connect, Amazon Lex V2, AWS Lambda, and Amazon Bedrock Agent — building end-to-end conversational IVR solutions that have achieved an 82% speech recognition rate and 60% containment on critical payment features. I've led teams of 5 engineers and mentored 50+ associates through the Connect Academy initiative.

NIT Raipur graduate (B.Tech, Mechanical Engineering). Python is my primary language; Rust my secondary. Outside engineering, I enjoy playing chess.
